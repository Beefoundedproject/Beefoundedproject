We are a Project organization dedicated to assisting elderly and incarcerated individuals in reclaiming their unclaimed property funds. Our mission is to provide support and resources to those who may not have the means or knowledge to navigate the process of recovering their lost assets.

Our services are free of charge, but we do accept donations.

Steps on searching and claiming your funds today!

🐝 STEP 1: Visit the Utah Unclaimed Property Website
https://mycash.utah.gov/
Look for the option to search for unclaimed property.

🐝 STEP 2: Enter Your Name
Enter your first and last name in the search boxes.
Helpful tip: Try searching your current name, maiden name, previous last names, or names used in the past.

🐝 STEP 3: Look Through Your Results
If your name appears, review the information carefully for previous addresses or businesses. Remember, not every result belongs to the person searching.

🐝 STEP 4: Select the Property That May Belong to You
If you recognize a listing, follow the instructions on the official Utah website to begin your claim.

🐝 STEP 5: Provide the Required Documents
Depending on your claim, the state may ask for proof of identity, previous address, or ownership. Only submit sensitive documents through official, secure state channels.

🐝 STEP 6: Submit Your Claim
Follow the instructions to submit your claim and keep your claim confirmation or claim number for your records.

🐝 STEP 7: Wait for the State to Review Your Claim
The State of Utah will review your information and contact you if anything else is needed.

🐝 STEP 8: Receive Your Money!
If your claim is approved, the state will arrange payment according to its procedures.

Congratulations! You may have recovered money that you didn't even know was waiting for you!
