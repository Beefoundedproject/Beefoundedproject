How Our Donation-Based Model Works

Many commercial services charge high fees to help you claim your own money. We keep our guidance completely free through community donations.

1

Search & Verify

Users search state registries with our easy interface to locate forgotten utility deposits, paychecks, or bank accounts.

2

Free Step-by-Step Guidance

We provide simplified instructions, document checklists, and direct links to official state portals without hidden fees.

3

Donor Supported

Our platform is sustained by community donations and successful claimants who choose to "pay it forward."
